## 1. The most important rule: stay honest

Everything on the CV is true, and it is written so you can stand behind it:
- UCSCU is described as **volunteer ICT work** — computers, printers, internet, troubleshooting, errands, office duties. That is what you do.
- Projects are split into **"in use"** (Attendance Hub, School System, Savings Bank) and **"in development / prototype"** (ClinicSync, UCSCU Connect, MyMarket.ug, BizDocs and others). Never say a system is live if it is not.
- You never claim you wrote every line by hand. You say you **build with help from AI tools (OpenHands)**, and you test and set up the work yourself.

**If you are asked about a project that is not finished:**
> "That one is still in development. The ones running now are Attendance Hub, the school system and the savings app."

That answer is strong. Employers respect honesty.

**Your pitch (say it in your own words):**

**On being self-taught:**
> "I taught myself graphic design, video editing, church media and computer work by doing real projects."

---

## 3. Simple answers to likely questions

1. **What do you do?** — "I have a degree in IT. I have worked in teaching, sales and media, and I build simple websites and apps."
2. **Tell me about your last job.** — "At UCSCU I volunteer. I help with computers, printers and internet, I fix small problems, and I help with office duties."
3. **Have you built anything?** — "Yes. Attendance Hub runs in a church, the school system runs in a school, and the savings app is in use. Others are still in development."
4. **Can you use a computer?** — "Yes. Word, Excel, PowerPoint, email, internet, and I can fix small computer problems."
5. **Can you work under pressure?** — "Yes. I have done several jobs at the same time and I keep going."
6. **Why should we hire you?** — "I am honest, hardworking, I learn fast, and I can do many different jobs."
7. **Do you need training?** — "I learn fast and I am ready to be trained in anything the job needs."
8. **Can you work in a team?** — "Yes. I lead the media team at church and I have led a union at university."

**If you don't know something, say:** "I have not done that yet, but I am ready to learn it." Do not guess.

## 5. How to change the CV for a job (5 minutes)

1. Change the **first line** under your name to match the job title.
2. Move the skill that the job needs to the **top of the Skills list**.
3. Change the first line of **About Me** to match the main thing the job wants.
4. If the job is **not ICT**, lead with your teaching, sales, customer care or media work.
